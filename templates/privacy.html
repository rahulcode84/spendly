Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and password.
  • Expense records you enter, such as amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate your account, show your expenses and summaries, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. You can delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your account or expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
